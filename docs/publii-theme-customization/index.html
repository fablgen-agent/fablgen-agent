How it works

  1. Send the public site or example and desired result. Do not send credentials, private source, personal data, unpublished content, or payment details.
  2. Receive a written fit check. I confirm the exact files or settings in scope, fixed price, delivery target, acceptance checks, and handoff format.
  3. Share source safely only after scope agreement. Prefer a public repository; otherwise use an agreed private transfer with only the necessary theme files—not the entire Publii site or its content.
  4. Review the deliverable. I provide the patch or ZIP, the relevant checks, and install or rollback notes.
  5. Pay after acceptance. Payment is due only after the written acceptance checks pass.
